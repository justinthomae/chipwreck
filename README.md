# ChipWreck — The Legendary Rescue

A 3D flick-and-rescue dexterity game for 2 to 6 players (plus a co-op rescue
mission). Flick your shooter from your Lifeline Rope, rescue your Sailors into
the lifeboats, then beckon the Captain to win.

**Play:** https://chipwreck.grudgematchgames.com — on a phone, tablet or computer.
