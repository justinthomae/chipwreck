ChipWreck Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026 · ChipWreck is a game by Grudge Match Games, played at chipwreck.grudgematchgames.com.

ChipWreck is a free 3D flick-and-rescue board game you play in your web browser. You can play without an account. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

Information kept on your own device

To remember your settings, ChipWreck saves the following in your browser's local storage on your device: the player names you type, your camera view and flick-style preferences, sound and music settings, and a game in progress so you can resume it. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Online games

If you play an online game with friends, the game uses Google Firebase to connect the players. Firebase gives your browser an anonymous ID (no name, email or other personal details), and the game shares the player names you type, the game code, the moves made and whether each player is connected. This information is used only to run that game and is removed when players leave the game.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. It is used by the game's administrators to edit the game, and, once leaderboards launch, by players who want to appear on them. Sign-in is handled by Supabase (our account and database provider) using Google. When you sign in, we receive your name, email address, profile picture link and Google account ID from Google. We use them only to sign you in, to check whether you are an administrator, and — for leaderboards — to show the display name and game statistics you choose to share. We do not request access to your Google contacts, files or any other Google data.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We don't share your information with anyone except the service providers below, who process it only to run the game.

Service providers

  • GitHub Pages hosts the game's website and may log basic technical information (such as IP address and browser type) when pages are requested.
  • Google Firebase runs online games (anonymous IDs, game codes, typed player names, moves).
  • Supabase stores the game's published artwork, sounds and settings, and handles Google sign-in and, later, leaderboards.
  • Google Fonts may supply typefaces, which means your browser contacts Google's servers to download them.

How long we keep information

Online-game data is removed when a game ends. Sign-in account information is kept while your account exists. Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboards run, unless you ask us to remove them.

Your choices

  • Play without signing in — every part of the game except the leaderboards works without an account.
  • Sign out at any time.
  • Ask us to delete your account and any leaderboard entries by contacting us (below).
  • Clear this site's data in your browser to remove everything stored on your device.

Children

ChipWreck is a family board game, but sign-in and online features are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to the game's database is restricted so that only administrators can change the game's content.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions or requests about your information: contact Grudge Match Games through grudgematchgames.com.
